# Product Overview

**Job Finders** is a comprehensive South African employment platform that connects job seekers with employers through intelligent matching and streamlined job placement services.

## Core Features

### For Job Seekers
- **Job Aggregation**: Scrapes jobs from major South African job sites (Career24, PNet, Indeed, Career Junction)
- **Smart Job Matching**: AI-powered job matching based on user profiles and CVs
- **User Profiles**: Comprehensive job seeker profiles with CV management
- **Application Management**: End-to-end application workflow with one-click applications

### For Employers
- **Company Registration**: Easy onboarding process for businesses of all sizes
- **Job Posting Platform**: Direct job posting capabilities with advanced targeting options
- **Company Profiles**: Professional employer dashboards with ATS (Applicant Tracking System) integration
- **Candidate Management**: Access to matched candidates and application tracking
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Insights into job posting performance and candidate engagement

### Shared Platform Features
- **Blog Integration**: Hashnode-powered career advice and industry insights
- **Local Market Focus**: Specifically designed for South African employment landscape
- **Multi-Source Access**: Combined job aggregation and direct posting ecosystem

## Target Market

- **Primary**: South African job seekers across all industries and skill levels
- **Secondary**: South African employers, SMEs, and large corporations seeking talent
- **Tertiary**: Recruitment agencies and employment services
- **Quaternary**: Career coaches and HR consultants

## Key Value Propositions

### For Job Seekers
1. **Comprehensive Coverage**: Access to jobs from multiple SA job boards plus exclusive direct postings
2. **Intelligent Matching**: AI-driven job recommendations based on skills and experience
3. **Streamlined Applications**: One-click applications with profile auto-fill

### For Employers
1. **Dual Sourcing Strategy**: Reach candidates from both aggregated and direct posting channels
2. **Targeted Recruitment**: AI-powered candidate matching and recommendation system
3. **Cost-Effective Hiring**: Competitive posting rates with advanced targeting capabilities
4. **Local Expertise**: Deep understanding of South African employment market and regulations

## Platform Ecosystem

Job Finders operates as a two-sided marketplace where:
- Companies can register, post jobs, and access a pool of qualified candidates
- Job seekers benefit from both aggregated opportunities and exclusive direct postings
- Advanced matching algorithms serve both sides with relevant connections
- Comprehensive analytics provide insights for optimized recruitment strategies
