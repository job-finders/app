Job Seekers

Everything you need to know about finding and applying for jobs on JobFinders.

  • Creating an account is simple:

    • Click "Register" in the top navigation
    • Select "Job Seeker" as your account type
    • Fill in your basic information (name, email, password)
    • Verify your email address
    • Complete your profile to improve job matching

    Once registered, you can start browsing jobs immediately and apply with one click.

  • Our AI-powered matching system analyzes several factors:

    • Skills: Your listed skills and experience
    • Location: Your preferred work locations
    • Salary: Your salary expectations
    • Job Type: Full-time, part-time, contract preferences
    • Industry: Your industry experience and interests

    The system learns from your application behavior and improves recommendations over time.

  • While you can browse jobs without a CV, most employers require one for applications. We recommend:

    • Upload your CV during profile creation
    • Use our built-in resume builder if you don't have one
    • Keep your CV updated with recent experience
    • Tailor your CV for specific applications when possible

    Having a complete profile with CV increases your chances of being noticed by employers.

  • Job alerts help you stay updated on relevant opportunities:

    • Go to your dashboard and click "Job Alerts"
    • Click "Create New Alert"
    • Set your criteria (keywords, location, salary, etc.)
    • Choose notification frequency (immediate, daily, weekly)
    • Save your alert

    You can create multiple alerts for different types of positions and modify them anytime.

Employers

Information for companies looking to post jobs and find qualified candidates.

  • Posting a job is straightforward:

    • Register as an employer or company
    • Complete your company profile
    • Click "Post a Job" from your dashboard
    • Fill in job details (title, description, requirements)
    • Set application preferences
    • Review and publish your job

    Free accounts can post up to 5 jobs per month. Premium plans offer unlimited posting.

  • We offer flexible pricing plans:

    • Free: 5 job posts per month, basic features
    • Basic: R299/month - 20 job posts, enhanced visibility
    • Professional: R599/month - Unlimited posts, ATS integration
    • Enterprise: Custom pricing - Full features, dedicated support

    All plans include candidate management tools and application tracking.

  • Our applicant management system helps you stay organized:

    • View all applications from your dashboard
    • Filter candidates by skills, experience, location
    • Rate and add notes to applications
    • Move candidates through hiring stages
    • Send messages directly through the platform

    You can also export candidate data and integrate with external ATS systems.

Account Management

Managing your JobFinders account, privacy settings, and profile information.

  • If you've forgotten your password:

    • Go to the login page
    • Click "Forgot Password?"
    • Enter your email address
    • Check your email for reset instructions
    • Follow the link to create a new password

    If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

  • Yes, you can switch account types:

    • Go to Account Settings
    • Click "Account Type"
    • Select your new account type
    • Complete any additional required information
    • Verify the change via email

    Note that switching may affect your current data and require profile completion.

  • To permanently delete your account:

    • Go to Account Settings
    • Scroll to "Delete Account" section
    • Read the information about data deletion
    • Confirm your decision
    • Enter your password to verify

    Warning: This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be deleted.

Technical Support

Troubleshooting common technical issues and platform functionality.

  • CV upload issues are usually due to:

    • File size: Maximum 5MB allowed
    • File format: Only PDF, DOC, DOCX accepted
    • Browser issues: Try clearing cache or different browser
    • Internet connection: Ensure stable connection

    If problems persist, try our online resume builder or contact support.

  • To improve loading speed:

    • Clear your browser cache and cookies
    • Disable browser extensions temporarily
    • Check your internet connection speed
    • Try using a different browser
    • Close other tabs and applications

    If issues continue, it may be temporary server maintenance. Check our status page.

  • To ensure you receive our emails:

    • Check your spam/junk folder
    • Add noreply@jobfinders.co.za to your contacts
    • Verify your email address in account settings
    • Check notification preferences in your profile
    • Ensure your email provider isn't blocking our emails

    Contact support if you're still not receiving important notifications.
